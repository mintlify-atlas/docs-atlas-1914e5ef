---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Get up and running with Snippet in minutes: sign in, create a group, upload a track, and share it with a friend."
---

This guide walks you through the entire Snippet workflow as a new user. By the end you'll have signed in, created a group, uploaded a track, invited a friend, and downloaded a track.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Go to the Snippet login page. You'll see a single button: **Continue with Google**.

    Click it. You'll be redirected to Google's OAuth consent screen. Select the Google account you want to use, grant access, and you'll be sent back to Snippet and land on your dashboard.

    <Note>
      Snippet uses Google OAuth exclusively. There is no email/password sign-up flow. You must have a Google account to use Snippet.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first group">
    On the dashboard, you'll see the **Your Groups** section. Since your account is new, it will show a **Create Your First Group** card.

    Click the **Create Group** button in the top right of the **Your Groups** section (or click the empty state card). A dialog will open asking for:

    - **Group name** — required, e.g. "Road Trip Mix" or "Band Practice"
    - **Description** — optional, a short note about what the group is for

    Fill in the name, then click **Create**. The new group card will appear on your dashboard immediately.

    <Tip>
      Group names don't have to be unique across Snippet — only your members will see your group name, so pick something that makes sense to them.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the group page">
    Click the group card on your dashboard. You'll be taken to the group page at `/groups/group?id=...`.

    The group page shows:

    - The group name and description in the header
    - A **Members** count and **Tracks** count
    - A **Shared Tracks** panel on the left (empty for now)
    - A **Members** panel on the right listing everyone in the group
    - Two action buttons: **Upload Track** and **Invite Members**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload a track">
    On the group page, click **Upload Track** in the header. The upload dialog opens.

    Fill in all four required fields:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Music File** | An MP3 or MP4 file, up to 50MB. Drag and drop onto the upload area or click **Select File** to browse. |
    | **Track Title** | The name of the track, e.g. "Golden Hour" |
    | **Artist** | The artist name, e.g. "JVKE" |
    | **Share with Group** | Select the group you want to share this track with from the dropdown. |

    Once all fields are filled, the **Upload Track** button in the dialog footer becomes active. Click it. A spinner appears while the file uploads to storage and the track record is saved.

    When the dialog closes, the track appears immediately in the **Shared Tracks** list on the group page.

    <Warning>
      Only MP3 (`.mp3`) and MP4 (`.mp4`) files are accepted. Other formats such as `.wav`, `.flac`, or `.ogg` will be rejected. The maximum file size is 50MB.
    </Warning>

    <Tip>
      You can also open the upload dialog from the dashboard. Tracks uploaded from the dashboard let you choose any of your groups from the **Share with Group** dropdown.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite a friend">
    On the group page, click **Invite Members** in the header. An invitation dialog will open.

    Copy the invite link shown in the dialog and send it to your friend — by text, email, or any other way.

    When your friend opens the link, they'll be taken to the Snippet login page with a redirect parameter already set. After they sign in with Google, they'll land directly inside your group as a full member.

    <Note>
      Your friend needs a Google account to accept the invite. As soon as they sign in, they appear in the **Members** panel of the group page.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download a track">
    Any member of a group can download any track in that group.

    On the group page, find the track in the **Shared Tracks** list. Each track row shows the title, artist, the email of the person who uploaded it, and the upload date. On the right side of each row there is a **Download** button.

    Click **Download**. The file will be fetched from Supabase Storage and saved to your device with a filename in the format `Artist - Title.mp3` (or `.mp4`).

    <Tip>
      Recently uploaded tracks from all your groups also appear under **Recently Uploaded** on the main dashboard. Click through to the group to download from there.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
