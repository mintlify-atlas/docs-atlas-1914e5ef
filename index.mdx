## What is Snippet?

Snippet is a private music sharing platform that lets you upload and share audio files with your friends inside closed groups. Rather than posting links publicly, you create a group, invite the people you trust, and everyone in the group can upload and download tracks freely.

Each group acts as a shared music library — you can see who uploaded what, search by title or artist, and download any track directly to your device.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create an account using your email address. Snippet uses Supabase Auth for secure authentication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or join a group">
    Create a new music sharing group or accept an invitation to join an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a track">
    Upload an MP3 or MP4 file, add the track title and artist name, and select the group to share it with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share and download">
    Every member of the group can see and download the tracks you share. Browse, search, and enjoy.
  </Step>
</Steps>
