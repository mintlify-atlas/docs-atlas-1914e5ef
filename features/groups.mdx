---
title: "Groups"
description: "Groups are private music sharing circles where members can upload, share, and download tracks together."
---

A group in Snippet is a private music library shared between a set of invited members. Every track you upload belongs to a group, and only members of that group can see and download it. You can belong to as many groups as you like — one for close friends, another for a band, another for a record club.

## Creating a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Create Group dialog">
    On your dashboard, click the **Create Group** button in the top-right corner of the "Your Groups" section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the group details">
    Enter a **Group Name** (required) and an optional **Description** to tell members what the group is about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Create Group**. The new group appears immediately in your dashboard grid, and you are automatically added as its first member.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The group name field is required. The description is optional but recommended — it helps invited members understand the group's purpose before they join.
</Note>

## The dashboard groups grid

All groups you have created or joined are displayed in the **Your Groups** section of the dashboard. Each card shows the group name and its description. Clicking a card takes you to that group's detail page.

If you have not joined any groups yet, the dashboard shows a **Create Your First Group** prompt in place of the grid.

## The group detail page

Clicking a group card opens its detail page. The page is divided into two areas:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Shared Tracks" icon="music">
    The main panel lists every track uploaded to the group. Each entry shows the track title, artist, the uploader's email address, and the upload date. A **Download** button is available for every track.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Members panel" icon="users">
    The sidebar lists all current members. Each member is shown with their username (derived from their email address), their avatar initials, and the date they joined the group.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The page header displays the **group name**, **description**, and two summary stats: the total number of members and the total number of shared tracks.

From the header you can also access the **Upload Track** and **Invite Members** actions.

## Multiple groups

You can be a member of any number of groups at the same time. The dashboard displays all of them. The **Recently Uploaded** section on the dashboard aggregates the latest tracks across every group you belong to, so you always have a single view of new music no matter which group it came from.
