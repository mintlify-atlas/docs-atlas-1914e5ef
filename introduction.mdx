Snippet is a private, group-based music sharing platform. Instead of public playlists or social feeds, Snippet organizes music around closed groups — circles of friends or collaborators who share tracks with each other directly. Every upload lives inside a group, accessible only to its members.

Authentication is handled entirely through Google OAuth, so there are no passwords to manage. Once you sign in, you can create groups, upload tracks, invite friends, and download anything shared by group members.

## Core concepts

### Groups

A group is a shared music library. You create a group, give it a name and optional description, and then invite members. All tracks uploaded to a group are visible and downloadable by every member. You can belong to multiple groups simultaneously.

### Tracks

A track is an uploaded audio file — either MP3 or MP4 — with a title and artist name attached. When you upload a track, you choose which group it belongs to. The track then appears in that group's **Shared Tracks** list, attributed to your account with the upload date.

### Members

Members are the people in a group. The person who creates a group is automatically a member. Additional members join through invitation links. The group page shows a members list with each person's username (derived from their Google account email) and the date they joined.

### Dashboard

The dashboard at `/dashboard` is your home view. It shows:

- **Your Groups** — every group you've created or joined, as clickable cards
- **Recently Uploaded** — the latest tracks from all your groups in reverse chronological order
- **Activity Feed** — coming soon

From the dashboard you can create a new group or jump directly to any group page.
